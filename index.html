Products

Cloakroom

Your agent reads your files. Some of them hold passwords, keys and customer names. Cloakroom swaps those for placeholders on your machine before the model sees them, then puts the real values back in the files the model writes.

It is free and open source. It works with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and Cursor.

About

I live in Charlotte, North Carolina. I've worked in IT since 2012, most of it building data platforms for aviation, banking, telecom, hospitality and retail. I'm now focused on agentic AI: building assistants that do real work, and making them reliable and private enough to trust with real data.

I like building things, and I find it hard to leave a working thing alone. A pipeline that runs should run without anyone watching it. A query that works can usually cost less. I go back over my own work more often than anyone asks me to.

Cloakroom started as a home project. I was setting up an AI model to run on my own machine, because I wanted my files to stay private. I wanted the same privacy when I used Claude Code, and nothing did it the way I needed. So I built it, and made it open source.

Most days I work in Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, Python and AWS. I hold the SnowPro Core and CKAD certifications, and a degree in mechanical engineering from Anna University.
