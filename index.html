Shanjeev Rajendran

Data platform architect. I build local-first tools that let people use AI on their real files without handing over their data.

Now

Cloakroom, a privacy plugin for Claude Code. It swaps secrets and personal data for placeholders before the model sees them and puts the real values back in what Claude writes. Runs on your machine, learns from your corrections, open source.

Source · How it works

Next: agentic and retrieval patterns for data platforms, built the same way: local first, benchmarked, open.

Background

Fourteen years building enterprise data platforms, at Tata Consultancy Services, Accenture, Altimetrik and now Ally in Charlotte. The recurring work: metadata-driven frameworks on Snowflake, dbt, Airflow and AWS that replace weeks of source-by-source engineering with configuration, and the CI/CD and testing around them. Led and mentored a team of five. SnowPro Core, CKAD. B.E. Mechanical Engineering, Anna University.

The two halves connect: the data that must never leave a bank's platform is the same kind of data people paste into AI tools. Cloakroom came from that.

How I work

Pain first, then the fix. I keep a running list of what gets in the way when working with AI tools, match it against what already exists, and build only where nothing fits. Everything ships with a benchmark anyone can rerun.
